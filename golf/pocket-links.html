Nine holes you can play between builds. Three shot games: a four part full swing, a touchy chip game around the greens, and a putting stroke of its own. Build your bag in myNolan and sharpen every shot in Training.

Three shot games. Point at the spot you want, then click (or press Space) once per part. Every part is scored out of 100.
  1. Full swing: Backswing, Tempo, Face, Low point. Squaring the face is hard, and the low point runs from a chunk to a topped ball. A Bump and run uses this swing too, with wider windows.
  2. Chip: Hands, Length, Contact. Pick a Low, Mid or High flight: low is easy and runs, high stops fast but is hard. Harder than a full swing, just like the real thing.
  3. Putt: Face, Length, Strike. Read the white streaks on the green, they drift downhill.
Keys. ← → fine aim (Shift for finer), ↑ ↓ or wheel change club, T shot type, 1 2 3 chip flight, V map, C scorecard, M sound, Esc cancel.
