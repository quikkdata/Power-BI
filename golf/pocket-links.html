Nine holes you can play between builds. Read the wind, pick your club, nail the three click swing. A fresh course every day, and a new one whenever you want.

How to swing. Point at the spot you want to land on (or drag on a phone). Every swing has four clicks, or Space four times, and each one is scored out of 100:
  1. Address. Click when the bubble on the level is centered. Off center opens or closes your aim.
  2. Backswing. The needle runs right. Stop it on the dashed line, your planned distance. Past the white line is overswing.
  3. Tempo. Pause at the top. Click when the bar fills to the yellow window. Rushed hooks left, lazy slices right.
  4. Low point. Click as the needle comes back through the yellow zone. Early is thin, late is fat.
Keys. ← → fine aim (hold Shift for finer), ↑ ↓ or mouse wheel change club, V hole map, C scorecard, M sound, Esc cancel swing.
